How FindIt Works

A secure and transparent process to reunite students with their belongings.

1. Post a Report

Submit a lost or found report with item name, category, campus location, date, and an optional photo.

2. Search & Match

Browse items by category, campus buildings, or keywords to discover matching posts immediately.

3. Verify & Reclaim

Submit a claim message with proof of ownership (e.g. serial number, unique marks) and pick up your item safely.
